# Product

## Register

product

## Users
One person: Alyan, an ASU student living at SoL Tempe who rides the apartment shuttle. He checks it one-handed on an iPhone, usually on his way out the door or standing at a stop in full Arizona sun, sometimes half awake in a dim room.

## Product Purpose
Answers one question faster than the Samsara share page can: when do I need to leave for the bus? It turns a raw dot on a map into a time, and tells him without being asked, through push alerts. Success is never checking the map just to find out he missed the bus.

## Brand Personality
Calm, certain, unfussy. Reads like a good transit app or Apple Weather: one clear answer, then detail if you want it. Plain language, never alarmist.

## Anti-references
Samsara's fleet-admin look (dark navy chrome around a map that says nothing about timing). Dashboards with stat tiles. Anything that makes him read before he knows the answer.

## Design Principles
- The answer comes first. The time to leave is readable at arm's length before anything else loads into view.
- Never overstate certainty. Estimates are labelled, and stale or missing data says so plainly instead of showing a confident number.
- Silence is a feature. Alerts fire once, when they change what he should do.
- The map supports the answer; it does not replace it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Legible in direct sunlight, so contrast is treated as a hard floor. Full reduced-motion support. Status is never carried by colour alone.
