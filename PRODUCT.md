# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People who live at SoL Tempe and ride the apartment's shuttle to ASU. Mostly students. They check it one-handed on a phone, usually on the way out the door or standing at a stop in full Arizona sun, sometimes half awake in a dim room. It began as one resident's own tool and each person's setup is still private to them.

## Product Purpose
Answers one question faster than the Samsara share page can: when do I need to leave for the bus? It turns a raw dot on a map into a time, plans the day around each person's own classes, and tells them without being asked, through push alerts. Success is never checking the map just to find out you missed the bus.

## Brand Personality
Calm, certain, unfussy. Reads like a good transit app: one clear answer, then detail if you want it. Plain language, never alarmist. The bus's own colours are the identity: its blue carries the answer, its orange means go.

## Anti-references
Samsara's fleet-admin look (dark navy chrome around a map that says nothing about timing). Dashboards with stat tiles. Anything that makes someone read before they know the answer. Anything that looks like it's run by the apartment or the university: it isn't.

## Design Principles
- The answer comes first. The time to leave is readable at arm's length before anything else loads into view.
- Never overstate certainty. Estimates are labelled, and stale or missing data says so plainly instead of showing a confident number.
- Silence is a feature. Alerts fire once, when they change what someone should do, and only around their time on campus.
- The map supports the answer; it does not replace it.
- Take as little as possible. No location, no tracking; the only counting is cookieless page views. A person's classes are theirs alone and one tap erases them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Legible in direct sunlight, so contrast is treated as a hard floor. Light and dark, by the person's choice. Full reduced-motion support. Status is never carried by colour alone.
